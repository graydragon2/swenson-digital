The Room

Built for sound, not for show

A converted freight warehouse with 38-foot ceilings, a line-array system tuned for the room instead of dropped in from a rental catalog, and a stage big enough for a six-piece without anyone stepping on a pedalboard.

  • Full line-array sound system, tuned monthly
  • Stage depth built for full bands, not solo acts squeezed into a corner
  • Full bar, craft cocktails, local beer on tap
  • Green room, load-in dock, on-site sound engineer
