Questions people ask

I don't have good photos of my business.

That's fine — I can shoot simple photos with my phone when I visit, or walk you through getting a few good ones yourself. You don't need a professional photographer to look legit online.

What if I want to make changes myself later?

You can always just ask — small edits are included every month. If you want more hands-on control, I can also set things up so you can edit basic info yourself.

What if I already have a website?

I can take over hosting and upkeep on your existing site, or rebuild it if it's outdated or hard to manage — whichever makes more sense for your business.

Will I show up when people search on Google or Google Maps?

Setting up (or cleaning up) your free Google Business Profile is part of every website build — that's the listing that shows your business on Google Maps and in local search results, at no extra cost. I don't promise specific rankings or timelines — nobody honestly can — but I make sure the basics are done right.

Do I own my website and domain?

Yes — always. The domain and the site are yours. If we ever part ways, they go with you, no questions asked.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract on the monthly services — cancel anytime. The one-time build fee is exactly that: one time, upfront, no surprises later.
