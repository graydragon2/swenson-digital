About Swenson Digital

It's just me — here's why that's the point.

Hi, I'm Robert Swenson — the only person behind Swenson Digital. I grew up right here in Wayne County, and I still live and work here now, at RYAM. Swenson Digital is something I do on the side — not because I have to, but because I kept noticing good local businesses getting overlooked online and wanted to do something about it.

Helping people around here isn't new to my family. My parents worked in real estate for years — first with Harris Real Estate, then running Swenson Rentals on their own. I grew up watching them build relationships with people one property and one handshake at a time. That's the same way I run this: I'm not trying to be a big agency, I'm trying to be useful to the people around me.

If you run a business around Wayne County and you're tired of feeling invisible online, that's exactly who I built this for.
